# AR RC Car

An augmented reality RC car simulator that runs in a phone browser.

Open the live page: https://upforwow-coder.github.io/ae-rc-car/

- Live camera mode drives a 1:32 RC car over your camera feed, tracked with phone motion.
- Room-scale AR (WebXR, Chrome on Android) anchors the car to your real floor.
- Photo and Studio floors work anywhere.
