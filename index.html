Pedals are analog: press low for a little, slide up for more; screens that sense touch pressure use it too. A Bluetooth game controller works (triggers for gas and brake, left stick to steer). Keyboard: arrows or WASD, space to brake, L for lights. Live camera and room-scale AR need camera access; if this viewer blocks it, use a photo of your floor, or open the standalone file from an https address in your phone's browser (Chrome on Android for room-scale AR).
